E-Sourcing

An e-sourcing web application for retail buyers and sourcing managers.

Web Application

More about the application

The E-Sourcing platform is a comprehensive procurement solution that connects retail buyers with suppliers. It streamlines the sourcing process by providing tools for requesting quotes, comparing supplier offers, managing contracts, and tracking procurement analytics. The platform was built to handle the complex workflows of retail procurement at scale, with a focus on usability for both buyers and suppliers.

Key features

Request for Quotes

Create and manage RFQs with structured supplier questions, deadlines, and automated reminders.

Supplier Comparison

Side-by-side comparison of supplier offers with scoring and weighted evaluation criteria.

Procurement Analytics

Real-time dashboards with spending insights, supplier performance metrics, and trend analysis.

Contract Management

Track and manage contracts from creation through renewal, with automated alerts and version control.

Supplier Portal

Dedicated portal for suppliers to submit quotes, manage documents, and track application status.

Role-based Access

Fine-grained permissions for buyers, suppliers, managers, and administrators with full audit trails.
