Mutual Non-disclosure & Confidentiality Agreement

1. Definitions

1.1 "Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other party (the "Receiving Party") in connection with the evaluation or performance of a potential business relationship, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically, or by any other means, that is designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.2 "Party" / "Parties" refers to the Disclosing Party and the Receiving Party, individually or collectively as the context requires.

1.3 "Term" means the period during which this Agreement is in effect, including any renewal period agreed upon by the Parties.

1.4 "Representatives" means, with respect to a Party, its directors, officers, employees, agents, consultants, advisors, and any other persons acting on its behalf who have a need to know the Confidential Information.

2. Purpose

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish the terms and conditions under which the Receiving Party may access and use Confidential Information provided by the Disclosing Party, solely for the purpose of evaluating or pursuing a potential business relationship between the Parties (the "Purpose"). The Receiving Party shall not use the Confidential Information for any other purpose without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.

3. Confidentiality Obligation

The Receiving Party agrees to:

  1. Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and protect it with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.
  2. Not disclose, copy, publish, or otherwise disseminate the Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.
  3. Restrict access to the Confidential Information to those Representatives who have a legitimate need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those set forth herein.
  4. Notify the Disclosing Party promptly upon becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Confidential Information and cooperate to remedy such unauthorized use or disclosure.

4. Safekeeping

The Receiving Party shall take all reasonable measures to safeguard the Confidential Information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. This includes, but is not limited to, implementing appropriate physical, electronic, and managerial safeguards to protect the Confidential Information. The Receiving Party shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

5. Dissemination

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to those Representatives who have a need to know such information for the Purpose and who have been informed of the confidential nature of the information. The Receiving Party shall be responsible for ensuring that each such Representative complies with the terms of this Agreement and shall obtain a written agreement from each such Representative to protect the Confidential Information in a manner consistent with this Agreement.

6. Exclusions

The obligations set forth in this Agreement do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

  1. Is or becomes generally available to the public without breach of any obligation owed to the Disclosing Party.
  2. Was lawfully in the Receiving Party's possession prior to disclosure by the Disclosing Party, without confidentiality obligations.
  3. Is lawfully received from a third party without restriction and without breach of any obligation to the Disclosing Party.
  4. Was independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Confidential Information, as demonstrated by the Receiving Party's written records.
  5. Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order, provided that the Receiving Party gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of such requirement (to the extent legally permitted) and cooperates with any efforts by the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

7. Return of Information

Upon the request of the Disclosing Party, or upon the termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy (at the Disclosing Party's election) all Confidential Information, including all copies, extracts, and summaries thereof, and shall certify in writing that it has done so. This obligation does not extend to information that the Receiving Party is required to retain under applicable law or its standard data retention policies, provided that such information remains subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement.

8. Breach

The Receiving Party acknowledges that any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm to the Disclosing Party for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. The Disclosing Party shall therefore be entitled to seek injunctive relief or other equitable remedy, without the necessity of posting a bond or proving actual damages, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity. The Receiving Party shall also be liable for all damages, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from any breach of this Agreement.

9. Term and Termination

This Agreement shall become effective upon acceptance by both Parties and shall remain in effect for a period of five (5) years from the date of such acceptance (the "Term"), unless earlier terminated by either Party in writing. The confidentiality obligations set forth herein shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement for the duration of the Term and shall continue to protect the Confidential Information disclosed during the Term.

10. No Obligation to Proceed

Nothing in this Agreement shall obligate either Party to proceed with any transaction, business relationship, or further discussions. Each Party reserves the right to reject or decline any proposal, project, or engagement, for any reason or no reason, without liability to the other Party. No contract, partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship shall be created by this Agreement or by the disclosure of Confidential Information.

11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Germany, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Ilmenau, Thuringia, Germany, for any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

12. Miscellaneous

12.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter herein and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous understandings, agreements, and communications, whether written or oral.

12.2 Amendments. Any amendment or modification to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

12.3 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid or unenforceable provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid and enforceable.

12.4 Waiver. No waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party. No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver of that right.
