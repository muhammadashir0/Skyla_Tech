Information Clause for Candidates

Pursuant to Article 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Skyla Tech provides the following information regarding the processing of personal data of candidates who submit applications to our company.

1. Data Controller

The data controller processing your personal data is:

Skyla Tech
Ilmenau, Thuringia, Germany
Email: career@skylatech.dev

You may contact the data controller in writing at the address above or by email.

2. Purpose and Legal Basis of Processing

Your personal data is processed for the following purposes:

  1. Application processing and recruitment — to evaluate your application, conduct interviews, and make hiring decisions. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent) and Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (contractual steps).
  2. Legal obligations — to comply with legal requirements related to employment, tax, and labor law. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal obligation).
  3. Archiving — to maintain records of applications for a limited period. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest).

3. Types of Data Processed

We process the following categories of personal data:

  • Identity data: First name, last name, date of birth.
  • Contact data: Email address, phone number, mailing address.
  • Professional data: CV, cover letter, previous employment history, education, qualifications, certifications, portfolio.
  • Recruitment data: Interview notes, test results, assessments, references.
  • Data you voluntarily provide: Any additional information you include in your application materials.

4. Storage Period

Your personal data will be stored for a period of up to 12 months from the date of submission of your application, unless you consent to a longer retention period or unless you request earlier deletion. If your application results in employment, your data will be processed in accordance with the employment agreement and applicable law.

After the retention period expires, your data will be deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

5. Data Recipients

Your personal data may be shared with:

  • Skyla Tech employees involved in the recruitment process, including HR personnel, hiring managers, and technical interviewers.
  • External service providers acting on our behalf, such as recruitment platform providers or background check services, bound by contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal authorities, where required by law.

Your data will not be transferred outside the European Economic Area without appropriate safeguards.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  1. Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR) — obtain confirmation and a copy of your data.
  2. Right of rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) — request correction of inaccurate data.
  3. Right of erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) — request deletion of your data.
  4. Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR) — request limitation of processing.
  5. Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR) — request transfer of your data.
  6. Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR) — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  7. Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR) — withdraw consent at any time.
  8. Right to lodge a complaint (Art. 77 GDPR) — file a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at career@skylatech.dev. We will respond within one month of your request, free of charge.

7. Provision of Data

The provision of your personal data is voluntary. However, failure to provide the required data may result in your application being unable to be processed or considered for the position.

8. Changes to This Clause

We may update this Information Clause from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this clause periodically.

9. Contact

If you have any questions or requests regarding the processing of your personal data, please contact us:

Skyla Tech
Email: career@skylatech.dev
Data Protection Officer: contact@skylatech.dev
Address: Ilmenau, Thuringia, Germany
