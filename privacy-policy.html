Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Skyla Tech ("the Company", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who interact with our website, use our services, or contact us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and protect personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws.

By accessing our website or providing us with your personal data, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to our data processing practices.

2. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for processing personal data collected through this website is:

Skyla Tech
Ilmenau, Thuringia, Germany

3. Data We Collect

We collect personal data that you voluntarily provide to us when you interact with our website or services. This includes:

  1. Contact form data: Name, email address, message content, and any preferences you select when submitting a form.
  2. Career application data: Name, email address, CV content, and any additional information you provide when applying for a position.
  3. Cookie and usage data: Information about how you interact with our website, including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on pages.
  4. Communication data: Emails and other correspondence you send to us.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds under GDPR:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Where you have explicitly given consent for a specific processing activity, such as receiving marketing communications.
  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): Where processing is necessary to provide our services or respond to your inquiries.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, such as website improvement, security, and analytics, provided these interests do not override your rights.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): Where processing is required to comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. How We Use Your Data

We use the personal data we collect for the following purposes:

  • Responding to inquiries and fulfilling requests submitted through our contact form.
  • Processing job applications and communicating about career opportunities.
  • Providing, maintaining, and improving our services.
  • Analyzing website usage to enhance user experience and functionality.
  • Sending administrative communications, such as service updates or policy changes.
  • Ensuring website security and preventing fraud or abuse.
  • Complying with legal obligations and enforcing our rights.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your data with:

  • Service providers: Third-party companies that process data on our behalf, such as email service providers, hosting providers, or analytics services. These providers are bound by contractual obligations to protect your data.
  • Legal authorities: Where required by law, by legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Skyla Tech, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of the transaction, subject to continued protection under this policy.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods depend on the type of data and the purpose of processing.

Contact form submissions are retained for the duration necessary to respond to your inquiry and any subsequent follow-up. Job application materials are retained for up to 12 months unless you request earlier deletion. Website usage data is retained for the duration of your visit and aggregated data may be retained for longer periods for analytical purposes.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, destruction, or accidental loss. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS/SSL.
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel.
  • Regular security assessments and monitoring.
  • Secure data storage and transmission practices.
  • Employee training on data protection obligations.

Despite these measures, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

9. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  1. Access (Art. 15): Request confirmation of whether we process your data and obtain a copy of it.
  2. Rectification (Art. 16): Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  3. Erasure (Art. 17): Request deletion of your data where processing is no longer necessary or where you withdraw consent.
  4. Restriction (Art. 18): Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  5. Portability (Art. 20): Request transfer of your data to another controller in a structured, commonly used format.
  6. Objection (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.
  7. Withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)): Withdraw your consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  8. Complaint (Art. 77): Lodge a complaint with a supervisory data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contact@skylatech.dev. We will respond to your request within one month, free of charge, unless the request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

10. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze traffic, and personalize content. Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our website. You can manage your cookie preferences through the cookie consent mechanism displayed on our website.

You can also set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain features of our website.

11. International Transfers

If we transfer your personal data outside the European Economic Area (EEA), we will ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision, to protect your data in accordance with GDPR requirements.

12. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of these external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or other factors. When we do, we will post the revised policy on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
